### Kings Courier - Orchestral Production

I was commissioned by the indie studio 'Goblinwatch' to produce the sountracks for their new game. The game itself is very inspired by the Might and Magic series and the goal was to achieve the orchestral sound of Paul Romero's style. 

**Insights:**
The inspiration for the music is much more on the romantic age and 90s-00s Hollywood orchestral than the modern Action RPC cliches, I explored modal scales, melodies with particular flavours with an orchestration hybrid between chamber music ensamble and full symphonic setup.
