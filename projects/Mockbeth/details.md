### Mockbeth - GlobalGameJam 2026

I curated the Soundtrack and Sound-Design of Mockbeth, a Co-op 2D puzzle game we made for the GlobalGameJam 2026. Moreover We wont the 1st prize in the local competition at the International Comics School of Florence! 


For the sounds I was pretty inspired from the graphical assets and I decided to mix a Professor Layton like Vibe with a more Medioeval/Baroque Theatrical Mood. 

**Tracks Created:**
*   **Menu Theme** A chimes bells loop introduces the spirit of the game, suggesting a puzzle Exeperience with a mystery to uncover. To go into a cohesive experience I used the same Melodic Cells ( Motif ) in all the tracks! 
*   **Epic Climber** This track is the most heroic one Used in the Platform like beginning tutorial. 
*   **Gameplay Thme** A relaxed Theme played by chamber instruments (violin/cello/harmonica/harpsichord) that heavly resembles the mood of the Investigation sections in Professor Layton games. 


**Time to Make:** Approximately 20 hours (3 days).
