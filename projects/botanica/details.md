### Botanica - Organic Sound Design Concept

''Sound Design Concept in Botanica Style, indomitable beauty''

I produced the sound design track by recording and synthesising a dynamic sonic scape in the style of Electronica Botanica. Featuring Foley recordings of squeezed vegetables, lush synthesizers pads, risers and sound effects, bottle poppings and much more. 

The video is a time lapse of flower growth from the National Geographic Youtube channel, edited and  visually composited with Touchdesigner.
